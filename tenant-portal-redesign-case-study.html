Tenant Portal Redesign

Turning a text-heavy tenant portal into a clearer self-service journey for prospects and tenants — from application through move-in and everyday living.

Role
Product & systems design · Implementation
Team
Solo — proposed, designed & implemented
Platform
StarRez PortalX
Status
Shipped · Late 2025
Scale
7 buildings + 40+ rooming houses

PortalX supported the journey from application through tenancy, but the experience relied heavily on long text templates. Prospects lacked clear guidance during application, while tenants still contacted Building Managers for information that should have been easier to find.
