Website Redesign

Redesigning a rental website to help prospective renters find the right room faster, while making property listings easier for Building Managers to update.

Role
Product, UX & delivery
Team
Solo, designed and delivered
Status
Shipped · 2025–2026
Approach
Iterative, ongoing releases

The original website showed available rooms and properties, but gave people little help deciding which room suited them. I used analytics and recurring enquiry patterns to identify where people needed more help, then redesigned the site around those points.

Start with what people already know

Renters arrive with different levels of knowledge. The website gives them a relevant starting point based on what they already know.
