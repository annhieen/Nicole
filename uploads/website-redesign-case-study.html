Website Redesign

Turning a basic accommodation website into a self-service discovery system — while making the content maintainable by the people closest to the properties.

Role
Product, UX & Delivery
Team
Solo, AI-assisted build
Status
Shipped · 2025–2026
Approach
Iterative, ongoing releases

The original website showed properties, but did little to help people work out what suited them. I used analytics, recurring enquiries and conversations with Building Managers and tenants to understand where people got stuck, then kept improving the site around those problems.
