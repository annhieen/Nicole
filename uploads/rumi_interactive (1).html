Rumi

Turning a fragmented room-finding journey into a structured, human-supervised recommendation workflow.

Role
Product & BA / Delivery lead
Team

Status

Timeline
3 weeks to build

RoomingKos had traffic, but few meaningful enquiries — and when Leasing did get one, working out which room actually suited the prospect could take 5–7 minutes of manual cross-referencing per enquiry.

What it looked like
We needed more enquiries.
→
What was actually happening
Prospects didn't know what to ask for — and once they did, Leasing couldn't process it fast enough.
How do we turn the way an experienced Leasing Manager makes a recommendation into a system — without removing the judgement that still belongs to them?
The question behind Rumi
What I learned from Leasing
  • Room matching relied on a mix of fixed business rules and contextual judgement.
  • The same customer input could mean different things depending on who was asking.
  • Some requirements couldn't be verified from the available data.
  • A recommendation still needed a clear human owner before it could become action.

How I thought about it

Click a step to inspect the reasoning underneath.

01 Follow the problem, not the feature
Inspect +
I didn't start with AI. Guided Enquiry solved an information problem, then exposed a processing bottleneck that led to Rumi. I see Rumi the same way — one step toward a more connected customer and operational system.
Vague enquiries→ Guided Enquiry→ Better inputs→ Processing bottleneck→ Rumi
NEXT — vision, not yet built
Rumi conversion→ CRM update→ Less duplicate work→ Better insight
Part of the same direction as Operational Hub, below.
02 Model the decision before automating it
Inspect +
I translated how Leasing matches a prospect to available rooms into a structured decision process. Clear constraints remove options that cannot work; viable rooms are compared against the prospect's needs; uncertainty stays visible; and AI helps reason across the strongest remaining options.
Prospect needs Student · Oct move-in · 12+ months · $400/wk · Monash Clayton · Female-only
Impossible → Remove Trade-off → Compare Unknown → Verify
03 Decide where human judgement belongs
Inspect +
Rumi narrows the decision space, but Leasing keeps control of the final recommendation and response. It surfaces evidence, trade-offs and unknowns, then prepares a prospect-ready message that Leasing can edit, regenerate or tailor with additional instructions before sending.

04 Design for what happens next
Inspect +
A recommendation still has to work in real operations. I designed for AI failure, incomplete evidence and overlapping team ownership — so every output has a safe, clear path to action.

The system doesn't need to know everything. It needs to know what it can decide, what it should surface, and when a person should take over.
