Tenant Portal Redesign

Reworking StarRez PortalX so prospects and tenants could find the information they needed more easily — without changing the platform itself.

Role
Product & Systems Design · Implementation
Team
Solo — proposed, designed & implemented
Platform
StarRez PortalX
Status
Shipped · Late 2025
Scale
7 buildings + 40+ rooming houses

PortalX was used from application through tenancy, but most of the experience relied on long text templates. Prospects had little visual guidance during application, while tenants still contacted Building Managers for basic information that should have been easier to find.

How I approached it
01 Organise the portal around the journey
Inspect +
I mapped what prospects and tenants needed from application through move-in, everyday living and renewal, then reorganised the information around those stages.
Apply→ Prepare to move in→ Move in→ Live→ Get help→ Renew / Vacate
02 Add the missing pre-arrival stage
Inspect +
The existing journey jumped from completed paperwork to tenancy. I added a pre-arrival section for check-in, building information and practical setup, especially for international students arriving in Melbourne for the first time.
Check-in Transport Banking SIM Support
03 Build a clearer experience within PortalX
Inspect +
PortalX mainly gave me text templates and images. I used Figma to design fixed visual panels, kept changing information in PortalX templates, and linked the panels to the relevant pages.
Fixed visual panels+ Dynamic PortalX content→ Live tenant experience

Figma was used for visual design. The final experience was built directly in PortalX.

04 Reuse the structure across buildings
Inspect +
The core structure stays consistent, while branding, property information, facilities, contacts and user access change by building.
